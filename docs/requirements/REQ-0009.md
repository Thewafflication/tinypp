# REQ-0009 — Lexical model

**Content type:** Requirement

**Status:** Proposed

**Source:** S2 [lex]; see [source register](../research/standards.md).

## Scope

Tiny++ hosted C++98 implementation and its controlled engineering records.
Allocation: M2. Target scope follows ADR-0003; intermediate subsets do not
establish full conformance.

## Requirement

The compiler shall recognize C++98 tokens and literals according to the
selected character-set policy.

## Rationale

Makes lexical model an explicit, reviewable part of the C++98 product baseline
instead of inheriting an unverified upstream assumption.

## Verification

**Method:** Test and review

**References:** [TC-0009](../testing/cases/TC-0009.tex).

Partition keywords and alternative tokens, identifiers and universal character
names, integer/floating/character/string literals including wide forms; accept
legal cases and diagnose malformed or unrepresentable cases as specified by
the policy.

## Relationships

- **Derived from:** Source above and stakeholder C++98 compiler objective.
- **Depends on:** [REQ-0001](REQ-0001.md); detailed standards allocation in REQ-0002.
- **Conflicts with:** No known conflict; deviations require impact review.

## Tailoring

None for a full conformance claim. Intermediate milestones may implement a
published subset without claiming that this requirement is verified.
